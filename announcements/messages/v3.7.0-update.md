**Some cave hamsters have inhaled too much redstone dust. Naturally, the treatment plan now includes sunlight, patience, and unsolicited flute music.**

Version 3.7.0 adds a new music disc and an unreasonable amount of fixy fixy attention.

### Main Highlights

- **Redstone Fever:** Rare cave hamsters can spawn aggressive, scarred, red-eyed, and dangerously energetic. Distract them with an Acorn Flute, lead them above redstone depth, and keep them in direct sunlight for three Minecraft days to cure them.
- **Acorn Flutes:** Craft Lush, Ember, or Harmony variants and play riffs that distract creepers and fevered hamsters. Look directly at an owned hamster within eight blocks while you have an open shoulder or head slot to call it over; repeated successful mounts train that hamster to respond faster.
- **The Final Note:** Help cure a fevered hamster with flute music, tame it, mount it, then make the killing throw against a charged creeper while someone is playing the flute to keep the creeper calm—all to get the new Acorn Music Disc.
- **Acorn Ring:** When two players both wear one, their pets won't attack each other. Even applies to vanilla dogs, cats, and horses if configured! It also prevents you from accidentally hitting your own pets. Wear it in your offhand or a supported Accessories, Trinkets, or Curios ring slot.
- **Fixy Fixy:** Cave spawning, water rescue, storage-crate retrieval, Hide & Seek frequency, shoulder-throw selection, cheek visuals, guidebook warnings, idle breathing, and several other systems received attention.

### Important Config Note

Existing configurations keep their saved world-generation values. If you want the corrected White and Pearl Rose hamster spawning defaults, open the World Generation config and follow the **Missing White & Pearl Rose Hamsters** restore-default instructions in the full changelog.

The **Hamster Tips** guidebook contains new Redstone Fever and Acorn Flute entries, plus more musical rodentry knowledge for anyone determined to turn veterinary care into a boss fight. Read the full [changelog](https://modrinth.com/mod/adorable-hamster-pets/changelog) here.
